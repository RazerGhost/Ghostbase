Most of my time lately goes into SHADE.

SHADE is a data engine that runs underneath client sites. Each client gets their
own instance, on their own server, with their own database.

Editors work in an admin app. Plugins add what a client needs, like
appointments or a shop. The site itself can be built in any framework, and it
reads its content over an API.

The client owns their instance. There are no licence keys and no outside
services. Another developer can take it over without asking me.

It isn't ready yet. Formats and APIs still change, and the code stays private
until the first stable release. There's a [project page](/projects/shade) for
now, and I'll write more when there's something to try.
