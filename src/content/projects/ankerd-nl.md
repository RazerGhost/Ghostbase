ankerd.nl is the site for [Gavin](https://github.com/Gavin132)'s convention
channel. It has videos, a blog and photos.

It's an Astro site. Videos are fetched from YouTube at build time, and a daily
rebuild keeps them current. The blog is edited through Keystatic, so nobody
has to open a code editor.

I built it in July and cleaned it up in September.

More in the devlog: [ankerd.nl](/devlog/2026-09-19-ankerd-nl).
