Ankerd Con is the app my group uses for convention trips. It covers rides,
meals, hotel rooms, shared expenses and the event calendar.

It's React, Vite and Tailwind on the front and FastAPI on the back. Supabase
handles Postgres and Discord sign-in.

[Gavin](https://github.com/Gavin132) started it and
[Ayoub](https://ayoublfatmi.nl/#home) built most of the app. I joined in
September. I rebuilt the navigation around trips, restyled the screens and
fixed two rounds of security findings.

The app itself is private to the group. The code is open.

More in the devlog: [Ankerd Con](/devlog/2026-09-19-ankerd-con).
