I'm Dimitri de Jong. By day I run [RG Digital](https://rg-digital.dev), building
web apps and sites for other people's businesses. This is the other half: one
place for everything that isn't client work, instead of a dozen profiles nobody
visits twice.

## What's actually here

A [devlog](/devlog), mostly, and most of what lands there is small and
unglamorous — a rate limiter, a Docker build trick, a chart that took three
attempts to stop lying. None of it is written to impress anyone. It's closer to
notes-to-self that happen to be public, and the useful part is usually the thing
I got wrong first.

The rest is what a person accumulates: [projects](/projects) written up properly,
a [watchlist](/watchlist), [listening history](/listens) going back further than
the site does, and the [gear](/gear) I actually use rather than the gear I'd like
to be seen using.

This site is the largest thing in that list and the one I keep rebuilding, so it
ends up being its own longest-running case study —
[its project page](/projects/razerghost-site) covers how it's put together.

## RazerGhost and RG Digital

RG Digital is the client-facing side, and it reads like it: case studies, a
pitch, a contact form. This one has none of that. Same person, different reason
for existing.
